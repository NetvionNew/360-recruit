02 · Eight measures

A number you canargue with.

A measure the job does not ask about carries no weight, and the screen says so.

Required skills

weight 38

The job’s must-haves found on the CV, each with the line it was seen on. A neighbouring skill is pointed out, earns nothing.

Experience

weight 18

Months of this kind of work against the job’s years; gaps and college years left out. Well above the band is a conversation, not a penalty.

Work described

weight 14

What the job involves, found in what the CV says the person did. A third of it is already a strong showing.

Line of work

weight 10

Whether the CV’s employers and towns are in the job’s trade: a tile factory in Morbi counts as tiles.

Qualification

weight 8

The highest degree found against the one asked for; a step below costs a little, not everything.

Desirable skills

weight 8

The nice-to-haves the job names, counted the same way as the must-haves.

Location

weight 7

Same city scores in full, the same region (NCR) nearly so; elsewhere is flagged as a relocation.

Availability

weight 7

Up to 15 days notice scores in full, 30 nearly so, 60 and beyond less; 90 days or more is flagged.

03 · Two readers, one score

The system reads every CV.The AI reads beside it.

The system reading

Always on

  • Rules and a library of 354 skills in 12 trades, plus your own skill names.
  • Experience counted job by job on a calendar: overlaps once, gaps and college years left out.
  • Name, e-mail, Indian mobile, city, notice, current and expected pay, degree, employers.

The AI reading

When your company switches it on

  • Must quote the CV for every claim; a claim it cannot quote is thrown out and counted.
  • Judges whether the person has really done this kind of work, which a keyword count can miss.
  • Told to ignore age, gender, religion, caste, marital status, photo and name.
  • Uses the provider your company chooses, with a monthly allowance shown on the page.

Both results stay on the application, so the card in the pipeline shows the pair. The arithmetic is always the system’s.

04 · Ways in

Every way a CV arrives,one board per job.

Six ways in, each ending as an application against a job.

Publishing a job puts it on your careers page with an apply form and an address of its own. The page carries the markup Google for Jobs reads, plus a jobs sitemap and a feed.

Whichever way they come in, a person is kept once: matched on e-mail, then phone, with their skills merged and one application per job.

05 · CV intake

A pile of CVs,sorted by job.

  1. 1

    Upload. Up to 300 CVs at once: PDF, Word, text, or a photo of a paper CV, read with OCR. Know the job? Pick it.

  2. 2

    Read. Always by rules and a library of 354 skills; by the AI too, once your company switches it on.

  3. 3

    Place. Under each open job, the CVs it fits best; then those that fit nothing well, kept in the talent pool; then the unreadable.

  4. 4

    Tick. A person ticks who goes into which pipeline. The machine adds nobody.

  5. 5

    It learns. Corrections are kept, listed under “What it has been taught”, and used on the next CV.

06 · Indian CVs

It reads the CVsIndia actually sends.

Pay in lakhs, notice in months

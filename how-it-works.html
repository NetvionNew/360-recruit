How it works

From requisition to roll,step by step.

01 · One line, vacancy to roll

Approve it once.Every step follows.

  • A job can only be published against an approved requisition.
  • Every move is recorded: who moved a candidate, when, from where to where.
  • The requisition counts each hire and closes itself at its headcount.
  • Hire creates the employee in 360 HRMS, with leave and onboarding set up.

02 · Ten steps

One line,ten steps.

Step 1 · HR or a recruiter

Raise a requisition

How many people, on what money, by when and why; it gets its own code and goes to Pending approval.

Step 2 · Approver

Approve it

Approve, hold, send back with a reason, or cancel: nothing is advertised on a vacancy nobody agreed to.

Step 3 · Recruiter

Write the job

Against the approved requisition: the description, the work, and the skills the score is worked out against.

Step 4 · Recruiter

Publish and share

The careers page with Google for Jobs markup, a QR code, an apply e-mail address, and LinkedIn and WhatsApp shares.

Step 5 · Candidates, recruiters

CVs arrive

From the careers page, by e-mail, a folder of up to 300 CVs, or typed in by hand; a person is kept once.

Step 6 · System, recruiter

Screen and sort

Each application scored on eight measures with the working shown; sort by fit, drag the best to Screening.

Step 7 · Recruiter, panel

Test and interview

A written test unlocked on a call, a recorded video interview, and a scorecard from each panellist.

Step 8 · Recruiter, candidate

Make the offer

Fixed, variable, bonus, joining date, expiry; each change is a new version, and accepting moves them to Offer.

Step 9 · HR

Hire

One press creates the employee in 360 HRMS, sets up leave and onboarding, and counts against the requisition.

Step 10 · HR, management

Read what worked

The hires register, nine reports, and hire rate by source over 180 days.

03 · Who does what

Four people,one record.

HR

  • Everything in recruitment
  • The only role that can press Hire and put someone on the roll
  • Job boards, intake settings and the sample pipeline

Recruiter

  • Any login given the hiring sections
  • Requisitions, jobs, CV intake, the pipeline, tests, offers
  • No access to the employee roll or pay

Panel member

  • Joins interviews from their own login
  • Sees only the interviews they are on
  • Adds a scorecard: recommendation, rating, feedback

Candidate

  • Applies from the careers page, a QR code or by e-mail
  • Joins tests and interviews from a link, nothing to install
  • Books a slot from your link; is told a paper was received

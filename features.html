01 · Capabilities

Sixteen capabilities.One line to the roll.

Requisitions and approval

  • Headcount, CTC band, location, work mode, priority, join date, reason
  • Own code, REQ-YYMM-NNN; later On hold, Closed or Cancelled; senseless moves refused
  • Once approved, only headcount, priority and date change; closes itself when filled
Draft
Pending approval
Approved

Jobs and the careers page

  • Written against an approved requisition; its skills drive the score
  • Publish, and it is on your careers page at its own address
  • Google for Jobs markup, a jobs sitemap and a feed

QR codes and sharing

  • A QR code for every published job, to download and print
  • Scanning it opens that job’s apply page
  • A LinkedIn post and a WhatsApp share from the job card

CVs by e-mail

  • One apply address per published job
  • Board read from the sender: Naukri, LinkedIn, Indeed and five more
  • A 30-day log, failures included, each with its reason
FiledAlready appliedNo CV attached

CV intake, 300 at a time

  • PDF, Word, text, or a photo of a paper CV, read with OCR
  • Each CV tried against every open job, best fit first
  • A person ticks who goes into which pipeline

A fit score with its working

  • Eight weighted measures, each with the CV line it came from
  • Strong 85+, Good 65+, Partial 40+
  • Pay pointed out, never scored

AI reading, when switched on

  • Its result sits beside the system’s, never instead of it
  • It must quote the CV; unquoted claims are thrown out
  • Told to ignore age, gender, religion, caste, marital status, photo, name

The pipeline board

  • A column per stage; drag a candidate along
  • Find by name, initials, phone, place or skill; sort by fit
  • Reject with a reason, kept with the application

Written tests

  • Ten ready-made papers, 121 questions, or write your own
  • Unlocked live; a new question order for every candidate
  • Answers never reach the browser; five window departures end it

Video interviews

  • In the browser, nothing to install, up to six people
  • A waiting room; recording from the booked time, shown to all
  • A transcript and a write-up quoted from what was said

Calendar, panel and booking

  • A month of interviews; a panellist sees only theirs
  • Panel nudged half an hour before, then every five minutes
  • Candidates book a free slot from your own link

Offers with versions

  • Fixed, variable, bonus, joining date and expiry
  • Each change is a new version; only the latest can be accepted
  • Accepting moves the candidate to the Offer stage
ReleasedNegotiatingAccepted

Hire, straight to the roll

  • HR presses Hire; the employee is created in 360 HRMS
  • Next employee ID, department, manager, joining date, monthly gross
  • Leave balances and a seven-item onboarding checklist set up

Overview and reports

  • Six live figures, the funnel stage to stage, ageing requisitions, stalled candidates
  • Nine ready reports, from stuck-in-a-stage to interview load per panellist
  • In Excel, or e-mailed on a schedule

The hires register

  • Every hire keeps its record: source, score, interviews, offers, days to hire
  • Hire rate by source over 180 days (sample, %)

05 · Data & trust

Your candidates’ data,kept like your own.

A database of your own

Recordings stay inside

Fair by instruction

Every move on record

02 · Questions

Straight answers,before you ask.

Does it post my jobs to Naukri, LinkedIn and Indeed?

Not by itself, and it does not pretend to. Those boards need a commercial agreement before any software can post to them. Put the job’s own apply address in your posting, and CVs that come back by e-mail are marked with the board, read from the sender: Naukri, LinkedIn, Indeed, Monster, Shine, TimesJobs, Apna and foundit. Your careers page carries the markup Google for Jobs reads, with no agreement needed.

Does a machine decide who gets hired?

No. The system reads every CV with rules and a skills library, and shows its working. If AI reading is switched on, its result sits beside the system’s; it must quote the CV for every claim, claims it cannot quote are thrown out, and it is told to ignore age, gender, religion, caste, marital status, photo and name. A person ticks who goes into which pipeline.

What is the fit score made of?

Eight measures, each with a stated weight: required skills, experience, the work described on the CV, the line of work, qualification, desirable skills, location and notice period. A measure the job does not ask about carries no weight. Expected pay is pointed out, never scored.

Can a candidate cheat the written test?

It is built to make that hard. The right answers never reach the candidate’s browser, each candidate gets their own order of questions and options, copying and right-clicking are off, and leaving the test window is counted. After five departures the test ends itself. The paper is marked the moment it is handed in.

Do candidates need to install anything for a video interview?

No. They join from a link in the browser and wait in a waiting room until someone lets them in. A room holds up to six people. Recording starts at the booked time, and everyone, the candidate included, can see that it is recording.

What happens when we press Hire?

HR presses it, and 360 HRMS creates the employee with the next employee ID, the department, designation and reporting manager from the requisition, and the joining date from the offer. Leave balances and an onboarding checklist are set up at the same time. The requisition counts the hire and closes itself once its headcount is met. Pressing Hire twice does nothing the second time.

Is 360 Recruit separate from 360 HRMS?

360 Recruit is the hiring side of 360 HRMS, included in its Growth and Enterprise packages. Everyone you hire lands on the same roll that runs attendance, leave and payroll. Plans and prices are on the 360 HRMS pricing page.

How is candidate data looked after?

Each company has a database of its own. Interview recordings open only for your staff after signing in, and a shared recording travels as a link that expires and can be revoked. Under the retention schedule for India’s DPDP Act, candidates who were never hired can be removed automatically 12 months after their last activity.

Can I see the pipeline on my phone?

Yes. The 360 HRMS phone app has a Recruitment screen: the hiring figures, where candidates are by stage, each job’s pipeline, and each candidate’s details, interviews, feedback and offers, with a button to call or e-mail them. Changes are made in the web portal.

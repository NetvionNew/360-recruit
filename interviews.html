Tests & interviews

Seen beforethey are hired.

A written test sat on a video call that nobody can copy from, and interviews in the browser that are recorded, transcribed and written up. No other tool to buy.

Written testsVideo interviews
01 · Written tests

A paper they sitwhile you watch.

Multiple choice, one question at a time, on a video call. No login: the link is their identity.

  • Unlocked on the call

    The link is a waiting room until you press Unlock; the clock starts when they begin.

  • Watched as it happens

    Minutes left, questions answered, every exit from the window. After five, it ends itself.

  • Marked at once

    Score, pass or fail, each answer beside the right one, and a breakdown by topic.

ASSESSMENTS · SITTINGS

On screen: the sittings list. Who is sitting now, with minutes left and answers given; who is unlocked, invited, or done with a score.

01 · The candidate’s screen

Nothing to copy,nothing to pass on.

It opens full screen, with copying, pasting and right-clicking switched off.

  • Answers never leave the server

    Not in the page, not in the network traffic: marked on the server at hand-in.

  • A new order for every candidate

    Questions and their four options are drawn afresh, so “question 4 is B” is worth nothing.

  • Nothing half-finished

    Out of time, the test is handed in for them and marked as it stands.

WHAT THE CANDIDATE SEES

On screen: the candidate’s test. One question with four options, the clock counting down, and a palette of all twelve questions.

01 · The papers

Ten papers,ready on day one.

121 questions across ten papers. Add one and it is copied into your list to edit as you please.

  • General aptitude
  • Everyday numeracy
  • English and communication
  • Field sales fundamentals
  • Customer service and the telephone
  • Accounts and GST basics
  • Computer and spreadsheet basics
  • Safety on a factory floor
  • Supervising people
  • HR and payroll basics

Or write your own: time allowed from 5 to 240 minutes, a pass mark, and four options per question. The candidate is told their paper was received; the result is yours to give.

02 · Video interviews

Interviews in the browser,on the record.

The recording starts at the booked time, once the candidate and someone from your side are both in.

  • Nothing to install

    A link in the browser. Up to six in a room, encrypted by the browsers themselves.

  • A waiting room

    On by default: the candidate waits until someone from your side lets them in.

  • Colleagues on the panel

    Up to four, from their own login: told in 360 HRMS, on their phone, calendar and chat.

INTERVIEWS ON VIDEO · NEW MEETING

On screen: booking a video interview. What it is, when, how long, who is coming, and what is kept.

02 · After the call

Recorded, transcribed,written up.

Recorded from the booked time

Picture and sound, sound only, or off. Whatever is chosen is shown to everybody, the candidate included, the whole time.

A transcript and a write-up

Each side’s browser transcribes its own microphone. The write-up lists what they claimed, what they gave real detail on, and what to ask, each quoted.

A link to watch it later

It expires (7 days by default, 90 at most), can be revoked and counts its opens.

Flags, never a verdict

A watched test or interview notes leaving the window or full screen, the camera turned off, or a second face: for a person to read.

03 · Calendar & panel

Nobody forgetsthe interview.

The interview calendar

A month at a glance. HR sees every interview; a panellist sees only the ones they are on. Still to happen, mine, no-shows and recorded, for the month.

Nudged until they join

The panel is nudged half an hour before and then every five minutes until they join. “Send the link again” puts it back in their chat.

A booking link

Set the hours a candidate may book, the slot length and a buffer, and send your link. A taken slot is never offered twice.

Moving the time sends the candidate the new one, and the calendar entry they already have moves rather than a second one appearing.

04 · Scorecards

Every panellist,on the record.

  • Schedule a round on the candidate’s card: stage, time, length, video, phone or onsite, a meeting link, the panel.
  • Each panellist adds a recommendation (Strong Yes to Strong No), a rating out of five and written feedback.
  • Done, no-show or cancelled is marked on the round, and the whole history stays with the application.
